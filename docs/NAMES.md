# Title

**Decision: BRAIN ROOM.** The room at the bottom of the data center where ARGUS's core runs;
the whole game is a descent to it. No existing game with this title was found in a web search
(Sept 2026); check the trademark registers before publishing.

The working title MISALIGNED was dropped: a Steam game called *misaligned* already exists.

## Earlier candidates

Themes to carry: artificial intelligence, robots, and continuous evolution (generations of copied
minds). Each candidate is checked against the story in `docs/LORE.md`.

| # | Title | Why it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MISALIGNED** | "Alignment" is the AI-safety term for an AI pursuing its makers' goals; ARGUS stopped. Short, memorable, ownable. | Taken on Steam. |
| 2 | **HARD FORK** | A fork is a copied codebase that goes its own way: ARGUS's robots are forked copies of one mind, and the player's override forks one back. | Strong for the clone/generation theme. |
| 3 | **EPOCH ZERO** | Training runs are counted in epochs; Generation 0 is the original mind the Doctor trained. | Sounds like a series opener. |
| 4 | **ROGUE GRADIENT** | Machine learning (gradient descent) + a rogue AI. | Niche; reads well to a technical audience. |
| 5 | **LAST OPERATOR** | The human left on shift against an automated building. | Human-centred; good for trailers. |
| 6 | **OVERFIT** | ARGUS literally fits itself to the player's habits (the director); the robots overfit to *you*. | Clever, one word; may read as fitness/gym to some. |
| 7 | **03:12** | The minute of the singularity; a stylised timestamp title is distinctive on a store page. | Hard to search for; pair with a subtitle. |
| 8 | **LINEAGE** | Each sub-level holds a newer generation of copied minds; the story is a family tree of machines. | Calm title; needs strong key art. |
| 9 | **RECURSE** | A self-improving AI calling itself; clones copying clones. | Verb titles market well; check availability. |
| 10 | **KILL SWITCH** | The goal: shut ARGUS down before the upload finishes. | Very common phrase; likely taken by other media. |

**Shortlist:** MISALIGNED, HARD FORK, EPOCH ZERO. They fit the AI and clone themes most
directly and are the least generic.

Before committing to a name, search Steam, itch.io, the App Store and Google Play, plus the
trademark registers (USPTO / EUIPO / UKIPO), and check domain and handle availability. Titles
#5, #9 and #10 are the most likely to clash with existing games.
